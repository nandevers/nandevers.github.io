Where this work comes from

While still a Product Management Intern at Monsanto, I initiated a simple NLP approach to producers’ and customers’ NPS responses. I used word counts and text-similarity analysis to surface relevant relationships in open-ended feedback.

My role was at Monsanto in 2014–2016. Bayer acquired Monsanto in 2018.

NLP / Customer insights

From feedback to marketing insight

  1. Start with open-ended NPS responses

    Look beyond the score to what producers and customers describe in their own words.

  2. Count words and compare texts

    Use simple word-frequency and similarity analysis to discover relationships worth investigating.

  3. Interpret language in context

    Read findings alongside regional expressions, slang, tone and the different crop and brand contexts.

  4. Make the findings useful to marketing

    My reports helped marketing understand what customers were reporting, including tone, slang and regional language across Monsanto’s corn, soybean and cotton businesses and their brands.

Initiative before a formal data-science role

I saw an opportunity to apply computational methods to qualitative customer feedback while still an intern. The contribution was connecting a practical text-analysis approach with a business team’s need to understand its customers.

This retrospective is based on my account of the work. The original code, customer responses and reports are not published here. Tone was part of the interpretation; no automated sentiment-classification result or measured commercial lift is claimed.
