Xper. From datasets to origination decisions.

I built MLflow-based software to connect the modeling process across credit products.

The work behind the tool

One workflow for the modeling lifecycle.

I developed Xper, MLflow-based software for orchestrating the modeling workflow across credit-origination products: dataset discovery, availability checks, cleaning, feature engineering, hyperparameter tuning and acquisition-funnel simulation.

The aim was to make the process easier to orchestrate and connect model experiments with acquisition decisions.

Retrospective based on my account of Xper. The original implementation and customer data are not published here.
