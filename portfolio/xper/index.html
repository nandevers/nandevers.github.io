The work behind the tool

One workflow for the modeling lifecycle.

I designed and developed Xper, MLflow-based experimentation software that brought dataset discovery, preparation, feature engineering, tuning and validation into one workflow across credit products.

The aim was to make the process easier to orchestrate and connect model experiments with acquisition decisions.
